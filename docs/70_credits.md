## TARS and its upstream foundation

TARS is developed in [rex688-png/TARS](https://github.com/rex688-png/TARS), with
canonical behavior-plugin sources in
[rex688-png/TARS-Plugins](https://github.com/rex688-png/TARS-Plugins).
