## Independent transactions

JournalFacts centralizes exact nonnegative integer payouts: TotalEarnings, including explicit zero, is authoritative; otherwise UC BaseValue + Bonus or Vista sum(BioData.Value + Bonus) is used only with complete values. Bonus is an additional component, included once in the credited total. No first-discovery multiplier or presumed second sale is introduced. Unknown amounts are explicitly unconfirmed. Visible amounts remain numeric; existing Phase 1 speech normalization is unchanged. Organic species names/counts remain in the event template.

Assistant respects enabled_game_events in selection and reply generation. Disabled/historic sales cannot trigger celebrations, even when another response is pending. Exact timestamped full-payload duplicates are filtered within a batch and after an attempted TTS reaction; the in-memory digest history retains at most 512 transactions. Different timestamps/payloads, even equal amounts or same-second distinct pages, remain eligible. Direct questions remain eligible when sale triggers are removed. This is reaction-attempt deduplication, not an audibility claim. Missing timestamps are deliberately not deduplicated because identical transactions can be legitimate. Session restart/older-than-window speech replay is not persistently prevented; normal historic replay is suppressed.

Expedition already independently accounts for both UC journal variants and organic sales, fingerprints complete events, sums actual Value + Bonus and archives on the established next-jump lifecycle. Its code/history is unchanged; existing accounting/rebuild tests passed. Source facts remain distinct even when a model composes one response to an intentional event batch; no combined payout is manufactured.
